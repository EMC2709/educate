## Where Educate Wins

1. **Extended answer practice** — Our killer feature. Medly does NOT help with 3/6/8-mark written responses. These carry the most marks in real GCSE exams. No competitor does this.

2. **Live math & diagrams** — KaTeX renders proper mathematical notation (fractions, square roots, subscripts). Mermaid generates process diagrams. Medly has been criticised for missing diagrams in questions.

3. **Price** — £4.99/month vs £9.99/month. £39.99/year vs £95.90/year. Educate is literally half the price.

4. **School pricing** — £2.49/student/year with teacher dashboard. Medly has no visible school/institutional pricing.

5. **Free tier is genuinely usable** — Full question bank access across all 26 subjects without paying. Medly's free tier is demo-only.

6. **WJEC support** — Medly doesn't support WJEC (Welsh board). We do.

7. **Open source potential** — Built on Next.js/React, can be self-hosted by schools. Medly is closed-source.

8. **AI accuracy** — Medly has Trustpilot complaints about AI giving wrong answers and failing to spot mistakes. Our AI uses Claude Sonnet with structured prompts and Zod validation.

---

## Where Medly Wins

1. **Native iOS app** — Smooth native experience. We're web-only (responsive but not native).

2. **Gamification** — Quests, leaderboards, streaks, home screen widgets. Engaging for students. We have this on our roadmap but it's not built yet.

3. **Broader exam coverage** — A-Level, IB, IGCSE, AP, SAT. We're GCSE only currently.

4. **Social features** — Add friends, weekly leaderboards, compete. We don't have this yet.

5. **Established brand** — 249 Trustpilot reviews, TikTok presence, Mumsnet discussions. We're pre-launch.

6. **Visual progress maps** — Island-style curriculum visualisation. More engaging than our list-based progress.

## Strategic Positioning

Medly is our closest competitor but they've left major gaps:

1. **They don't do extended answers** — this alone is a compelling reason to switch
2. **They charge double** — £9.99 vs £4.99
3. **They have no school offering** — we can sell to schools, they can't
4. **Their maths is weak** — our KaTeX rendering is genuinely better
5. **Their diagrams are missing** — our Mermaid integration fills this gap

**Our pitch against Medly**: "Everything Medly does, at half the price. Plus the one thing no revision app does — AI-marked extended answer practice for the questions worth the most marks."
