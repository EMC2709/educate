## ROI Analysis for Calday

### Teacher Time Savings

| Activity | Current time | With Educate | Saving |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Marking homework (per teacher/week) | 5-8 hours | 1-2 hours (review AI marks) | 4-6 hours |
| Creating practice questions | 2-3 hours/week | 0 (AI generates) | 2-3 hours |
| Identifying struggling students | Manual review | Automated dashboard | 1-2 hours |
| **Total per teacher/week** | **~12 hours** | **~4 hours** | **~8 hours** |

With ~60 teaching staff, that's potentially **480 hours/week** of teacher time returned to actual teaching.

### Student Outcome Projections

| Metric | Without Educate | With Educate (projected) |
|--------|----------------|------------------------|
| Extended answer practice | Classroom only | Unlimited AI-marked practice |
| Revision app usage | 3-5 fragmented apps | 1 unified platform |
| Feedback turnaround | Days (teacher marking) | Instant (AI marking) |
| Personalised support | Limited (30:1 ratio) | 24/7 AI tutor per student |

### Cost Comparison vs Alternatives

| Solution | Annual cost (450 students) | Coverage |
|----------|---------------------------|----------|
| **Educate (Option B)** | **£1,120** | **All subjects, AI tutor, extended answers** |
| Seneca Premium (per student) | £3,825 (£8.50/student) | Quiz-only, no extended answers |
| Tassomai (3 subjects) | ~£6,750 (£15/student) | Science only, MCQ only |
| GCSEPod | ~£3,600 (£8/student) | Video only, no interactive |
| Save My Exams (per student) | ~£5,400 (£12/student) | Notes + basic AI marking |
| CGP revision guides (all subjects) | ~£6,750 (£15/student) | Paper only, no feedback |

**Educate is 3-6x cheaper than alternatives while offering more features.**
